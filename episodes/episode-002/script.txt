At 3:17 in the morning, a city camera caught a man pointing at an empty building. He wasn't looking at the street. He was pointing at the fourth floor. The guard checked the entrance. The doors were locked, the hallway was empty, and every window was dark. Then the camera showed the same man inside. He stood at the end of the corridor, facing the same place, completely still, as if he had been waiting there for someone who never arrived. Police replayed the footage, but found no entrance, no footsteps, and no reflection in the glass. Twelve minutes later, the building collapsed. The recording was supposed to end there. But one frame was recovered from the backup drive. It showed a hand pressed against a glass door. In the corner, the timestamp read three eighteen. The next morning, the camera turned on again. This time, it was inside a different building, aimed at another empty floor.
