At two fourteen in the morning, a hospital elevator stopped at a floor the building did not have.

The guard on duty checked the panel. There was no button for thirteen. The system log still showed the doors open for forty-seven seconds.

Then the camera found a corridor beyond the doors: narrow concrete, one red emergency lamp, and no sign of construction. Elias stepped forward. The feed went black.

By morning, maintenance had found no thirteenth landing. The control board was replaced. The elevator was chained shut.

At two fourteen the next night, the display returned to thirteen.

This time, the camera stayed on. A figure in Elias's uniform stood at the far end of the red corridor.

The lobby monitor showed Elias outside the elevator, watching. Then the system added a second passenger.

The indicator began descending below the basement. No one pressed a button.
