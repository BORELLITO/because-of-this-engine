At exactly 3:17 every morning, the security camera inside an abandoned hotel recorded the same figure standing behind the glass door.

The strange part was not the face. It was the fact that the door opened into a room that had been sealed for eleven years.

A night guard finally decided to wait beside it. For twenty minutes, nothing moved. Then the lights went out.

When they came back, the figure was no longer behind the glass. It was walking at the end of the hallway, closer than before.

The guard ran outside and called the police. They found no footprints, no broken lock, and no person inside the building.

But the camera had captured one final detail.

At 3:18, the figure lifted one hand and pointed directly at the camera.

The hotel was demolished the next week. The recording was deleted.

But every night at 3:17, the same camera still turns on.
