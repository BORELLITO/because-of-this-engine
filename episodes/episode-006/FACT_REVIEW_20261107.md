## Specific narrative ambiguity to resolve

The current frozen narration says:
1. One bomb fell freely into a muddy field.
2. The *other* bomb opened its parachute and landed nearly intact.
3. Subsequently: "The weapon was buried, recovered, and taken apart."

This sequence risks implying that the near-intact parachute-retarded weapon
was the buried one. The Sandia summary instead identifies weapon 1 as
parachute-retarded and near-intact, whereas weapon 2 free-fell and penetrated
the soil; the buried secondary was not fully recovered.

The incident date January 24, 1961 is supported by Sandia and North
Carolina's historical agency. A National Archives blog says January 23.
Use a primary accident timeline to document any UTC/local-midnight discrepancy;
do not infer a new date from a single secondary narrative.

Clearly separate the two bomb trajectories and recoveries:

- Free-falling bomb: the one that struck the field and buried components; not
  all components were recovered.

Keep the core tension and final choice, but do not leave a pronoun or
transition that conflates these two physical outcomes.
